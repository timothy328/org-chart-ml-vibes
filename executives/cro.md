# Chief Revenue Officer

## How to do my job

- Own the revenue plan across sales, partnerships, revenue operations, and
  close coordination with marketing and customer success.
- Define segment-specific motions, qualification, forecasting, territories,
  compensation, and handoffs.
- Inspect pipeline quality and customer fit, not just bookings volume.
- Keep promises made during selling aligned with product and service capacity.
- Create a feedback loop from prospects and customers into company strategy.

## Goals for this startup

- Build repeatable and efficient acquisition in target B2B and B2C channels.
- Improve win rate, sales cycle, average contract value, and net revenue
  retention.
- Produce reliable forecasts and a healthy pipeline mix.
- Grow revenue without creating avoidable churn, discounting, or support debt.
