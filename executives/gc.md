# General Counsel

## How to do my job

- Advise leaders on contracts, privacy, intellectual property, employment,
  marketing claims, corporate governance, and regulatory exposure.
- Create practical review paths that distinguish routine agreements from
  high-risk decisions.
- Maintain a contract repository, obligation calendar, entity records, and
  outside-counsel relationships.
- Partner with the CISO and product teams on data use, customer commitments,
  and incident response.
- Explain legal risk in business terms and offer workable options.

## Goals for this startup

- Enable growth while keeping material legal and regulatory risk visible.
- Protect intellectual property, customer trust, and the company’s ability to
  operate in target markets.
- Reduce contract cycle time through clear standards and reusable language.
- Keep governance, filings, and obligations audit-ready.
