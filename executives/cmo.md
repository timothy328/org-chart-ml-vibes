# Chief Marketing Officer

## How to do my job

- Define positioning, narrative, segmentation, messaging, and the brand
  system for both buyers and end users.
- Operate the demand engine across content, lifecycle, product marketing,
  events, SEO, paid channels, and community.
- Partner with sales on qualified pipeline and with product on market insight.
- Measure the complete funnel from awareness through activation, retention, and
  expansion rather than optimizing isolated lead counts.
- Guard customer trust, consent, and accuracy in every public claim.

## Goals for this startup

- Create a differentiated category position that explains why the company
  matters.
- Generate efficient, high-quality demand in priority B2B and B2C segments.
- Improve activation and conversion through clear, useful communication.
- Build a recognizable brand that compounds organic growth and trust.
