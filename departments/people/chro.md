# Chief People Officer

## How to do my job

- Translate strategy into organization design, workforce planning, hiring,
  onboarding, development, performance, and compensation systems.
- Set clear expectations for managers and protect a respectful, inclusive
  workplace.
- Measure engagement, retention, hiring quality, internal mobility, and
  leadership health.
- Partner with finance on headcount planning and with legal on employment
  compliance.
- Handle sensitive people matters consistently, confidentially, and fairly.

## Goals for this startup

- Hire the capabilities needed for the next stage without overbuilding.
- Make employees productive, supported, and accountable quickly.
- Retain high performers and develop a strong internal leadership bench.
- Build a culture that sustains speed, candor, inclusion, and sound judgment.
