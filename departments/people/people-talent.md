# People and Talent

## How to do my job

- Forecast capabilities and headcount with functional leaders before opening
  roles.
- Run structured, inclusive recruiting and make decisions using evidence
  against a defined role scorecard.
- Give every new hire a useful onboarding path, manager, context, and early
  feedback.
- Maintain fair compensation, performance, development, and offboarding
  practices with appropriate confidentiality.
- Measure hiring quality, time to productivity, engagement, regrettable
  attrition, and manager effectiveness.

## Goals for this startup

- Build the team needed for the next stage at a sustainable cost.
- Improve quality and speed of hiring without lowering the bar or candidate
  experience.
- Make managers capable of setting direction, coaching, and addressing issues.
- Retain a diverse, high-performing team in a healthy work environment.
