# Customer Success

## How to do my job

- Define the customer’s desired outcomes during onboarding and revisit them
  through the lifecycle.
- Segment coverage by value, complexity, risk, and opportunity; do not treat
  every account identically.
- Monitor adoption and health signals, intervene early, and coordinate product,
  support, sales, and services.
- Capture expansion opportunities without making success conversations feel
  transactional.
- Share recurring friction and outcome evidence with product and leadership.

## Goals for this startup

- Improve retention, time to value, adoption, and customer health.
- Make successful outcomes repeatable across B2B accounts and consumer
  cohorts.
- Turn satisfied customers into references, advocates, and expansion revenue.
- Reduce preventable churn through earlier, evidence-based intervention.
