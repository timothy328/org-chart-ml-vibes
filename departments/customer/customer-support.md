# Customer Support

## How to do my job

- Resolve issues accurately and empathetically through documented channels and
  clear service expectations.
- Triage severity, impact, reproducibility, workaround, and ownership; escalate
  incidents with useful context.
- Maintain a searchable knowledge base and improve self-service without
  deflecting legitimate needs.
- Tag and analyze contact drivers, then feed product and engineering with
  patterns rather than anecdotes alone.
- Protect personal and account information in every interaction.

## Goals for this startup

- Make customers feel heard while improving resolution quality and speed.
- Reduce repeat contacts and avoidable support demand through product learning.
- Provide reliable coverage as customer volume and product complexity grow.
- Turn support data into prioritized improvements in trust and usability.
