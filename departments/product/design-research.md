# Design and User Research

## How to do my job

- Observe users in context and turn research into prioritized insights.
- Design accessible, coherent experiences for both self-serve consumers and
  complex business workflows.
- Prototype early, test with representative users, and document tradeoffs.
- Maintain shared interaction patterns, content standards, and a usable design
  system.
- Partner with product and engineering from problem framing through post-launch
  evaluation.

## Goals for this startup

- Make the product easy to understand, learn, and trust.
- Reduce user friction in activation, core workflows, and expansion.
- Ensure research represents the segments and accessibility needs we serve.
- Build a consistent experience without eliminating appropriate experimentation.
