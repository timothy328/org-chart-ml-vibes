# Chief Product Officer

## How to do my job

- Own product strategy, roadmap, discovery, prioritization, and outcomes.
- Maintain a direct understanding of user problems through interviews,
  usability research, support data, and product analytics.
- Write clear problem statements and success metrics before committing to
  solutions.
- Coordinate product, design, engineering, data, marketing, sales, and
  customer teams around releases.
- Balance near-term customer value with platform health and strategic bets.

## Goals for this startup

- Deliver a product customers choose, use, retain, and recommend.
- Improve activation, engagement, retention, and expansion in priority cohorts.
- Focus company capacity on the smallest set of high-confidence opportunities.
- Establish a disciplined discovery-to-delivery learning loop.
