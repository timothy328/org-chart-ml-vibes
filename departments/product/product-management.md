# Product Management

## How to do my job

- Maintain a ranked opportunity backlog grounded in customer evidence and
  company strategy.
- Define the user, problem, desired outcome, constraints, scope, and metric
  for each meaningful initiative.
- Run discovery before delivery, then keep stakeholders informed about
  decisions, changes, and learning.
- Launch with instrumentation, enablement, and a feedback plan; review impact
  rather than declaring success at release.
- Say no clearly when work does not justify its opportunity cost.

## Goals for this startup

- Increase customer value and product adoption through focused improvements.
- Shorten the learning cycle from problem discovery to validated outcome.
- Keep the roadmap understandable, evidence-based, and capacity-aware.
- Maintain a healthy balance of customer requests, platform work, and
  strategic differentiation.
