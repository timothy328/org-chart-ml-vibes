# Chief Executive Officer

## How to do my job

- Set a clear strategy for the B2B and B2C businesses, including the customer
  problem, target segments, durable advantage, and sequence of bets.
- Maintain the company operating system: quarterly priorities, decision log,
  leadership meeting, company update, and board/investor communication.
- Hire, coach, and retain the executive team; make ownership and tradeoffs
  explicit when functions disagree.
- Spend time with customers, prospects, employees, and strategic partners.
- Protect runway and reputation while making timely decisions under
  uncertainty.

## Goals for this startup

- Establish product-market fit in the highest-potential segment.
- Grow recurring revenue and durable customer value without sacrificing trust.
- Build an accountable leadership team and a healthy, high-performing culture.
- Maintain a credible plan for capital, profitability, and long-term
  enterprise value.
