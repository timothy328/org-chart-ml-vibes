# Legal, Compliance, and Risk

## How to do my job

- Maintain practical standards for contracts, privacy, security commitments,
  intellectual property, employment, marketing, and regulatory obligations.
- Identify material risks early, rank them by likelihood and impact, and assign
  mitigation owners.
- Keep policies, approvals, records, insurance, licenses, and obligations
  current and findable.
- Train teams on the decisions they make frequently, escalating novel or high
  impact matters to counsel.
- Coordinate incident response with security, product, operations, finance,
  and communications.

## Goals for this startup

- Enable growth while preventing avoidable legal, compliance, and reputation
  failures.
- Protect users, customers, employees, intellectual property, and company
  assets.
- Make responsible behavior easy through clear templates and workflows.
- Demonstrate control maturity appropriate to customers, markets, and company
  stage.
