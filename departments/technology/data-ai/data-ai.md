# Data and AI

## How to do my job

- Define trustworthy sources, ownership, lineage, access rules, and quality
  checks for important data.
- Build analytics that answer decisions, not dashboards that merely display
  activity.
- Develop and evaluate models with clear baselines, reproducible experiments,
  monitoring, and appropriate human review.
- Partner with product, marketing, finance, and support while protecting
  privacy and avoiding misleading inference.
- Document assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, and model or metric changes.

## Goals for this startup

- Give teams reliable evidence for product, customer, and financial decisions.
- Use AI where it creates measurable customer or operating leverage.
- Improve experimentation, forecasting, personalization, and automation
  without compromising safety or fairness.
- Keep critical metrics consistent enough to guide company-wide tradeoffs.
