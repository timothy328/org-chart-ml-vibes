# Chief Technology Officer

## How to do my job

- Set the technology strategy with the CPO: architecture, quality,
  scalability, developer experience, and build-versus-buy decisions.
- Create engineering standards for planning, code review, testing, release,
  observability, incident response, and technical documentation.
- Keep technical investment connected to customer outcomes and business
  constraints rather than novelty.
- Partner with the CISO on security and with the CFO on infrastructure cost.
- Develop engineering leaders and maintain a realistic delivery forecast.

## Goals for this startup

- Ship reliable product improvements at a predictable pace.
- Build a platform that supports B2B scale and B2C usability.
- Reduce critical defects, outages, security exposure, and avoidable technical
  debt.
- Make technology a defensible advantage while keeping unit economics healthy.
