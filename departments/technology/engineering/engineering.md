# Engineering

## How to do my job

- Plan in small increments, clarify acceptance criteria, and keep work visible.
- Produce maintainable code with reviews, automated tests, observability, and
  safe deployment practices.
- Own services in production, including documentation, on-call, alerts,
  incident response, and follow-up work.
- Collaborate early with product, design, data, security, and support.
- Invest deliberately in reliability and technical debt when they affect
  customer outcomes or delivery speed.

## Goals for this startup

- Deliver valuable, reliable software at a sustainable pace.
- Improve deployment confidence, lead time, service health, and developer
  experience.
- Keep critical systems understandable, observable, and recoverable.
- Create a technical foundation that supports growth without premature
  complexity.
