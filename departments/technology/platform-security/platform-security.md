# Platform and Security

## How to do my job

- Operate cloud infrastructure, identity, networking, CI/CD, observability,
  backups, and disaster recovery as products for internal teams.
- Apply least privilege, secure defaults, patching, secrets management, and
  continuous vulnerability remediation.
- Track reliability and cost by service; make capacity and architecture
  decisions before they become emergencies.
- Test incident, rollback, restore, and access-review procedures.
- Work with engineering and legal to meet customer commitments and applicable
  controls.

## Goals for this startup

- Keep services available, recoverable, secure, and cost-efficient.
- Reduce time to detect, respond to, and learn from failures.
- Make the paved path the safest and fastest way to ship.
- Produce credible evidence of security and operational controls.
