# Chief Information Security Officer

## How to do my job

- Establish the security program across product security, corporate IT,
  identity, privacy controls, vulnerability management, and incident response.
- Maintain risk-based policies, control ownership, security training, and
  evidence for customer or regulatory reviews.
- Build security into architecture and development without blocking reasonable
  experimentation.
- Test detection, backup, recovery, access reviews, and incident playbooks.
- Report material risks and remediation status to the executive team and board.

## Goals for this startup

- Protect customer data, company systems, and business continuity.
- Earn trust in enterprise sales through credible, measurable security
  practices.
- Reduce exploitable vulnerabilities and time to detect and contain incidents.
- Make secure behavior a default part of product and company operations.
