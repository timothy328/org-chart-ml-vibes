# Sales

## How to do my job

- Qualify for a real problem, decision process, champion, economic buyer, and
  mutual timeline before forecasting an opportunity.
- Run discovery that teaches the buyer something useful; demonstrate outcomes
  rather than feature volume.
- Keep CRM records current, document commitments, and coordinate clean
  implementation handoffs.
- Segment the motion for enterprise, mid-market, SMB, and consumer-assisted
  channels where appropriate.
- Share objections, losses, and market signals with product and marketing.

## Goals for this startup

- Win customers with strong fit and a credible path to measurable value.
- Improve conversion, deal quality, sales cycle, and forecast accuracy.
- Build repeatable playbooks without turning conversations into scripts.
- Minimize discounting and expectations that create downstream churn.
