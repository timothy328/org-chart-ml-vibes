# Partnerships

## How to do my job

- Select partners based on strategic fit, customer value, reach, capability,
  economics, and execution readiness.
- Define the joint customer problem, responsibilities, commercial model,
  enablement plan, and success metrics before launch.
- Coordinate legal, security, product, sales, marketing, and support
  dependencies.
- Review partner performance and customer feedback regularly; exit weak
  relationships respectfully.

## Goals for this startup

- Create incremental distribution, product value, or defensibility.
- Generate measurable sourced or influenced revenue and retention.
- Make integrations and co-selling reliable for customers and internal teams.
- Avoid partnerships that add complexity without a clear strategic return.
