# Marketing

## How to do my job

- Start from audience, problem, positioning, and desired behavior for every
  campaign or content asset.
- Coordinate brand, product marketing, demand generation, lifecycle,
  community, events, and communications around one narrative.
- Test channels with explicit hypotheses, cohorts, budgets, and stop criteria.
- Attribute carefully; distinguish influence, causation, and correlation.
- Keep messaging accurate, accessible, privacy-respecting, and useful.

## Goals for this startup

- Create qualified demand and informed product adoption efficiently.
- Strengthen awareness and trust in priority B2B and B2C segments.
- Improve conversion throughout the funnel without relying on dark patterns.
- Build reusable content, channels, and customer proof that compound over time.
