# Revenue Operations

## How to do my job

- Own the definitions, systems, workflows, and reporting that connect
  marketing, sales, partnerships, and customer success.
- Keep lifecycle stages, territories, forecasts, compensation inputs, and CRM
  data consistent.
- Diagnose funnel leakage and propose process or enablement improvements.
- Automate repetitive work carefully, preserving auditability and useful human
  judgment.

## Goals for this startup

- Give revenue leaders a trusted view of pipeline, conversion, capacity, and
  retention.
- Reduce manual work and conflicting definitions across go-to-market teams.
- Improve forecast accuracy and speed from qualified opportunity to value.
- Make the revenue engine scalable without hiding poor customer fit.
