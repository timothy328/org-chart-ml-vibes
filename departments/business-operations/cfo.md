# Chief Financial Officer

## How to do my job

- Own the financial model, budgeting, forecasting, cash controls, and board
  reporting.
- Establish pricing, packaging, unit economics, revenue recognition, and
  approval controls with the commercial and product teams.
- Give leaders scenario-based answers about growth, hiring, investment, and
  runway.
- Ensure accurate books, tax filings, audits, payroll coordination, and
  responsible vendor spend.
- Make financial information understandable and timely for decision makers.

## Goals for this startup

- Preserve sufficient runway while funding the highest-return growth bets.
- Improve gross margin, retention economics, contribution margin, and
  forecast accuracy.
- Build finance infrastructure that can support diligence, audits, and scale.
- Make every major investment traceable to a measurable business outcome.
