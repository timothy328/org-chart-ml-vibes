# Business Operations

## How to do my job

- Turn executive priorities into cross-functional plans with milestones,
  dependencies, owners, and measurable outcomes.
- Run operating reviews that focus on decisions and exceptions, not status
  theater.
- Maintain a source of truth for key metrics, initiatives, risks, and decisions.
- Improve processes by observing real work, testing small changes, and
  measuring results.
- Escalate unresolved tradeoffs with options and a recommendation.

## Goals for this startup

- Increase organizational throughput and clarity as the company scales.
- Reduce duplicated work, blocked decisions, and avoidable handoff failures.
- Ensure strategic initiatives produce visible outcomes.
- Help leaders spend more time on high-leverage decisions and customers.
