# Chief Operating Officer

## How to do my job

- Translate strategy into cross-functional plans, owners, milestones, and
  operating metrics.
- Run the company cadence: quarterly planning, weekly business review,
  escalation management, and postmortems.
- Remove friction across product, sales, customer, finance, and people teams.
- Define repeatable processes without adding bureaucracy that slows learning.
- Monitor service quality, capacity, vendor performance, and operational risk.

## Goals for this startup

- Make execution predictable as headcount and customer volume grow.
- Improve cycle time from decision to shipped outcome.
- Ensure customers receive a consistent experience across B2B and B2C channels.
- Surface cross-functional risks early and close them with accountable owners.
