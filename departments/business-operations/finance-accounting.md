# Finance and Accounting

## How to do my job

- Record transactions accurately, close on a dependable schedule, and
  reconcile cash, receivables, payables, payroll, and revenue.
- Maintain a rolling forecast with assumptions that owners can explain.
- Enforce spending authority, vendor review, purchase orders where needed, and
  separation of duties.
- Report key metrics such as burn, runway, gross margin, ARR, cash collection,
  and unit economics with clear definitions.
- Prepare tax, audit, investor, and board materials on time.

## Goals for this startup

- Preserve financial accuracy and cash visibility.
- Support responsible growth with timely planning and decision support.
- Improve collections, margins, controls, and forecast accuracy.
- Keep the company ready for financing, diligence, and audit scrutiny.
