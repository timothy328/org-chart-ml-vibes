# Org Chart ML Vibes

A practical operating playbook for a generic, well-funded B2B and B2C
technology startup. The structure is designed to stay useful as the company
grows from an early product team into a multi-function organization.

## How to use this repository

- Start with the executive role that owns the decision.
- Read the functional department playbook before creating a new process.
- Use the subteam playbooks for day-to-day operating expectations.
- Revisit goals quarterly as strategy, customers, and company size change.

Every playbook answers two questions:

1. **How do I do my job?** — responsibilities, operating cadence, interfaces,
   and decision rights.
2. **What are my goals for this startup?** — measurable outcomes that connect
   the role to company strategy.

## Operating principles

1. **Customer value first:** prioritize retention, outcomes, trust, and
   willingness to pay over activity metrics.
2. **One accountable owner:** collaborate broadly, but assign one directly
   responsible individual for every important outcome.
3. **Evidence over opinions:** use customer research, product data, financial
   models, and operational facts to make decisions.
4. **B2B and B2C with one platform:** share foundations where it helps, while
   respecting different buying motions, privacy needs, and user experiences.
5. **Move quickly without creating hidden risk:** document decisions, protect
   customer data, and make reversible experiments cheap.
